Privacy Policy

Chicago RP Bot

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Chicago RP Bot provides moderation, administrative tools, event recording, and in-game functionality for the Chicago RP community.

1. Information We Store

The bot may store limited Roblox account information, including Roblox usernames and user IDs, as well as relevant event records needed to provide its features.

The bot is not intended to collect personal information unrelated to its functionality. Its actual data practices depend on the features enabled by the server administrators.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To support moderation and administrative functions.
  • To record and review relevant in-game events.
  • To provide Chicago RP community features.
  • To maintain the bot's functionality and security.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' information. Information may be accessed by authorized server staff or shared with service providers when necessary to operate the bot, comply with legal obligations, or protect the service and its users.

4. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the bot's features and maintain relevant event records. Information that is no longer needed may be deleted.

5. Data Requests

Users may request access to or deletion of information stored by the bot by contacting the Chicago RP server staff or bot administrator through the community's official support channels. Requests are handled subject to applicable requirements and legitimate operational needs.

6. Third-Party Services

The bot interacts with Discord and Roblox. These platforms have their own terms and privacy policies. Chicago RP Bot is not officially affiliated with or endorsed by Discord or Roblox unless explicitly stated.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Chicago RP server staff or the bot administrator through the official community support channels.
